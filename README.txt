Brainrot Heist 3D
=================
Open index.html in any modern browser to play.
Note: needs an internet connection (loads Three.js r128 from cdnjs).

Controls
- Move: WASD (or touch-and-hold left side on mobile)
- Look: drag the right side
- E: buy, steal, or use your base console/elevator
- F or slot 1: swing the bat
